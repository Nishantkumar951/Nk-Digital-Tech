#### Frequently Asked Questions

**What does a digital marketing company in Delhi NCR actually do?**

---

**How is SEO different from paid advertising?**

SEO (search engine optimisation) improves your visibility in organic, unpaid search results by making your website more relevant and authoritative for specific search queries. Paid advertising places your business at the top of results for a fee — and stops the moment you stop paying. SEO builds long-term visibility that continues to perform without ongoing ad spend. For most businesses, a combination of both works well, but SEO provides the more sustainable foundation.

---

**What is AI SEO and why does it matter for my business?**

AI SEO helps businesses improve their visibility on AI-powered search platforms like Google's AI features, Gemini and Perplexity. As more people use AI assistants to research businesses and make decisions, companies that have prepared their digital presence for AI-driven discovery have a meaningful advantage. AI SEO involves improving content quality, entity information, topical authority and brand signals so AI systems can understand and confidently recommend your business.

---

**What is Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)?**

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) is the practice of structuring your website content so that search engines and AI platforms can easily extract and display your information in response to specific user questions. This includes writing clear, direct answers to common customer queries, using proper heading structures, and including FAQ sections with conversational, question-based content. Businesses that invest in AEO are better positioned to appear in featured snippets and AI-generated answer formats.

---

**What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)?**

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the process of improving how your brand appears in AI-generated responses and recommendations. When someone asks an AI platform for business recommendations in a specific category or location, GEO increases the likelihood that your business is mentioned. It involves building a strong, consistent, and well-referenced online presence with authoritative content, accurate entity information, and credible digital signals.

---

**How long does SEO take to show results?**

SEO typically shows meaningful results within 3 to 6 months for competitive queries, though some improvements — such as technical fixes and local SEO updates — can show results faster. The timeline depends on factors like your starting position, the competitiveness of your industry, the quality of your website and how consistently the strategy is implemented. SEO is a long-term investment, and businesses that stay consistent see compounding returns over time.
